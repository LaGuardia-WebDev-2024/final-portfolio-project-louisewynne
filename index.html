About Me

Hello, I am Louise Wynne and I am a Junior fine arts major at Fiorello H. Laguardia High School. I love to oil paint, figure draw, and do ceramics. I predominately use oil paints, but would like to experiment more in the future.
Outside of school, I love reading, embroidery, playing bass guitar, and making friendship bracelets. I also love to bake, especially different kinds of cookies.
